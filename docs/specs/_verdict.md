## Closest adjacencies, adjudicated
1. **Hub cream #f4ebe0 vs 02 greige #EAE9E6** is the nearest canvas pairing in the lab (both light warm-neutrals; night pair #171310 vs #181818 also close). Resolved: the greige is measured law (rgb(234,233,230)), and separation is carried by radius (32px vs 0), type (chunky grotesk vs razor serif), and accent systems (toy-box cobalt/amber/violet vs pistachio/jade). No change.
2. **01 & 03 both run hairline-stroke drawn glyphs** — resolved by referent and weight (1.25px bone film-registration brackets vs 1px currentColor museum-plaque arrows). No change.
3. **01 & 03 share expo.out/power4 ease names** — resolved by tempo (0.35–0.65s snap vs 0.8–1.2s gallery) and application (hairlines/clips vs wipes/curtains). No change.
4. **01 & 02 both devote S4 to self-exhibited measurement** — the lab's shared ethos, but the gesture repeats in adjacent worlds; guardrailed via amendments (evidence-locker vs type-foundry register).
5. **02 & 03 both use seeded portrait canvas cards with index + caption** — same asset class, different language (physical paperwork collage vs type-monumental flat prints); the strongest echo in the set. Guardrailed via amendments so execution cannot drift together.

## Day/night set — reads intentional, no amendment
Hub both (sweep), 01 dark-only, 02 both (fade), 03 both (blackout), 04 both (press+fade) is a curated statement, not indecision: 01 is the permanent-night anchor that makes the four mode-aware worlds read as a choice. Every dual-mode world reads 'hub-theme' pre-paint and writes back (lab continuity), while each toggle speaks its own language — engraved sun/crescent (02), WHITE/NIGHT museum joke with blackout curtains (03), a physical pushbutton that enacts the thesis (04). 01's refusal of the key is the strongest stance in the set: cinema has no daytime, and arriving from a day-mode hub into the dark is a designed beat.

## Failure-mode scan — clean
No kindergarten 3D (04's hall is fillet-disciplined with the clay-vs-product env retune; the pole itself is premium toy-pop). No gradient blobs (02's chrome ramp is banded with a hard horizon; 01's fallback headlight glows are in-fiction light sources per the Sundial-fallback lesson). No keyword-fidelity builds (every section cites measured numbers; derivations like 03's fog range 7→14 are honestly marked OURS and computed from camera math). Highest-risk cheesy moment is 04's finale confetti — contained by palette discipline (three colors, green reserved for the stamp) and by being one burst in a 5-state journey; passes.

## overlaps

Found and resolved: (1) 01 General Sans = hub text face — RESOLVED by register, no change: hub runs it 400 sentence-case body on cream, 01 runs it 700 uppercase crushed to lh 0.80 on charcoal, and the measured SuisseBPIntl stand-in is law (v1 already shipped it); forcing 01 to Switzer would collide with 02 instead. (2) 02 greige vs hub cream — nearest canvases in the lab; RESOLVED by radius/type/accent separation, greige is measured law. (3) 02 & 03 seeded portrait canvas plates with index+caption — same asset CLASS; RESOLVED with hard guardrail amendments: 02 keeps physical-paperwork collage (numerals ≤64px engraved, process captions, tooth+jitter, rotation), 03 keeps type-monumental flat prints (620px numeral must bleed off-canvas, zero rotation/border/shadow/paper texture). (4) 01 & 03 hairline drawn glyphs — RESOLVED by referent/weight (1.25px film registration vs 1px museum plaque). (5) 01 & 03 shared expo.out/power4 ease names — RESOLVED by tempo (0.35–0.65s vs 0.8–1.2s) and application. (6) 01 & 02 S4 self-specimen sections — RESOLVED with register guardrails: 01 = evidence locker (alpha ladder, weight stack, spec rows; no oversized type), 02 = type foundry (vw ladder + one live ease demo; no alpha/material chips). (7) 02 & 04 both use the hub's 800ms cubic-bezier(0.45,0,0.55,1) theme fade — deliberate lab handshake, kept. (8) Hub Sundial relief and 04 hall share renderer grammar (lit primitives, one shadow light, NeutralToneMapping) — RESOLVED by experience: wall poster vs ridden machine; the hub's red peg/gate red = Pulse's red is intended gate-color continuity, not a violation. (9) 04 pill radius 44 vs hub pill 999 — both stadium; RESOLVED by scale and register (small hairline tags vs 54–64px CTA furniture).
